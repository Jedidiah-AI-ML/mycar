---
title: Fuel vapour (EVAP) leaks and the fuel cap
codes: [P0440, P0441, P0442, P0446, P0455, P0456, P0457, P0443, P0449, P0451, P0452, P0453]
systems: [evap]
keywords: [evap, cap, leak, vapour, purge, vent]
---
The EVAP system stops petrol vapour escaping from the tank. By far the most common cause
of these codes is the fuel cap: not clicked shut after filling, missing, or with a worn
seal. Tighten it until it clicks; the light usually goes off by itself after several
drives if that was the cause. If it comes back, cracked vapour hoses or a faulty purge or
vent valve are next. These codes don't affect how the car drives.
