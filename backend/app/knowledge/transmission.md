---
title: Automatic transmission codes
codes: [P0700, P0705, P0710, P0715, P0720, P0730, P0740, P0741, P0750, P0755, P0218]
systems: [transmission]
keywords: [transmission, fluid, gear, shift, slip, converter, solenoid]
---
P0700 only says the transmission's own computer has stored a fault; a scan tool that reads
the transmission module shows the real code. Old, low or burnt transmission fluid is the
most common root cause, and stop-and-go traffic in heat works the fluid hard. Slipping
gears or late, harsh shifts are signs to get it checked soon: a small fix early (fluid, a
solenoid) avoids a costly rebuild later.
