---
title: Oil pressure
codes: [P0520, P0521, P0524]
systems: [oil]
keywords: [oil, pressure, pump, level, sensor]
---
Low oil pressure can destroy an engine within minutes. If the oil pressure light comes on,
or this code appears with it, stop safely, switch off and check the oil level. A faulty
pressure sensor or switch can also set the code, but that should only be assumed after a
mechanic has checked the oil level and the real pressure.
