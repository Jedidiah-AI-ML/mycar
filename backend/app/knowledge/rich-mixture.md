---
title: Rich mixture codes
codes: [P0172, P0175, P2188]
systems: [fuel_air]
keywords: [rich, injector, pressure, purge, filter, coolant]
---
A rich code means too much fuel for the air. Leaking injectors, a fuel pressure regulator
letting too much pressure through, a purge valve stuck open, or a very blocked air filter
are the usual causes. A coolant temperature sensor that wrongly reports a cold engine also
makes the computer add fuel. A rich engine wastes fuel and, left long enough, can damage
the catalytic converter.
