title: Throttle body and idle speed

systems: [throttle, idle_speed]

---
Carbon builds up in the throttle body over time, especially with lots of idling in
traffic, and makes the idle unsteady or low. Cleaning the throttle body is cheap and often
fixes idle codes. On electronic throttles a fault can put the car in reduced-power limp
mode to stay safe; a throttle stuck open is a safety issue, so the car shouldn't be driven
until it's checked.
