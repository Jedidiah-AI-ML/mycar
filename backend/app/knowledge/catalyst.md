---
title: Catalytic converter efficiency
codes: [P0420, P0430, P0421]
systems: [emissions]
keywords: [catalyst, converter, oxygen, sensor, exhaust, leak, misfire, oil]
---
This code says the catalytic converter isn't cleaning the exhaust as well as it should,
judged by the oxygen sensor after it. Before replacing a converter (expensive), check the
cheaper causes: a failing downstream oxygen sensor, or an exhaust leak near the sensors.
Converters usually wear out because of something else, such as long-term misfires, a rich
mixture or an engine burning oil, so a new converter without fixing that cause fails
again. The car usually drives normally with this code.
