---
title: Variable valve timing and cam/crank codes
codes: [P0010, P0011, P0012, P0013, P0014, P0016, P0017, P0018, P0021, P0335, P0336, P0340, P0341, P0345]
systems: [cam_crank]
keywords: [timing, vvt, oil, chain, cam, crank, sensor, solenoid]
---
Variable valve timing is moved by engine oil pressure, so low, old or wrong-grade oil is a
frequent cause of these codes; an oil change with the right grade sometimes clears them. A
sticking oil control valve (solenoid) comes next. Correlation codes (P0016 to P0018) can
mean a stretched timing chain, which gets worse and can damage the engine, so they deserve
a prompt check. A failed crank sensor can make the engine stall or refuse to start.
