---
title: ABS wheel speed sensors
codes: [C0035, C0040, C0045, C0050, U0121]
systems: [brakes]
keywords: [abs, wheel, speed, sensor, brake]
---
Wheel speed sensors tell the ABS how fast each wheel turns. Mud, brake dust and damaged
wiring near the wheels are common causes of these codes. When ABS switches off, the normal
brakes still work, but the wheels can lock in a hard stop, so brake gently and get it
checked soon.
