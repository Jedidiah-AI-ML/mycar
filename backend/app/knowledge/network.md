---
title: Lost communication (U) codes
codes: [U0001, U0073, U0100, U0101, U0121, U0140, U0155]
systems: [network]
keywords: [communication, network, module, voltage, battery, ground, wiring]
---
U codes mean the car's computers lost contact with each other. A weak battery, poor ground
connections or corroded connectors cause many of them, so checking battery voltage and
grounds comes first. Water getting into connectors and rodent-chewed wiring are other
common causes. If the engine computer is the one missing, the engine may stall or not
start.
