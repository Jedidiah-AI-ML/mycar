Oxygen sensors measure leftover oxygen in the exhaust so the computer can fine-tune the
fuel. They age and slow down over the years, faster with fuel additives, oil burning or
coolant leaks. Heater codes mean the sensor's built-in heater (which gets it working
quickly after a cold start) has failed: often a sensor replacement, sometimes a blown fuse
or a damaged wire. Many newer cars use a wide-range air-fuel ratio sensor upstream
instead. A failing sensor wastes fuel slowly but rarely stops the car.
