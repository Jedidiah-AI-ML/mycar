Lagos traffic is hard on cooling systems: long idling in heat leaves the radiator relying
entirely on the electric fan. A fan that doesn't switch on (failed relay, fuse or motor),
low coolant from a slow leak, a radiator clogged with debris, or plain water used instead
of proper coolant are common causes of overheating. If the engine overheats, stop safely
and switch off: driving on can warp the cylinder head. Never open a hot radiator cap.
