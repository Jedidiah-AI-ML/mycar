---
title: Thermostat stuck open
codes: [P0128, P0125]
systems: [cooling]
keywords: [thermostat, warm, cold, coolant, temperature, open]
---
The thermostat keeps the engine at its working temperature. When it sticks open the engine
runs too cool, especially at speed, and takes long to warm up: more fuel used, a weak
heater, and this code. In Lagos's heat the engine may look normal in traffic and only run
cool on open roads, so the fault can come and go. A thermostat is a cheap part; a failing
coolant temperature sensor can give the same code, so it's worth checking both.
