---
title: Misfires
codes: [P0300, P0301, P0302, P0303, P0304, P0305, P0306, P0316]
systems: [ignition]
keywords: [misfire, spark, plug, coil, injector, compression, fuel]
---
A misfire is a cylinder that didn't burn its fuel properly. Worn spark plugs and failing
ignition coils are the most common causes; on many engines a mechanic can swap the coil
from the misfiring cylinder to another and see if the misfire follows it. Clogged or
leaking injectors, vacuum leaks and poor-quality fuel come next; low compression is less
common but serious. If the check-engine light FLASHES, the misfire is bad enough to
overheat and damage the catalytic converter, so the car shouldn't keep being driven hard.
