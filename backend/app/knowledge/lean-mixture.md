---
title: Lean mixture codes on cars in Lagos
codes: [P0171, P0174, P2187, P0170, P0173]
systems: [fuel_air]
keywords: [lean, vacuum, leak, air, fuel, pump, filter, injector, maf]

A lean code means more air than fuel is reaching the engine. In hot climates the rubber
vacuum and intake hoses harden and crack with age, so a hissing hose or a split intake
boot is a common first find. Dusty roads and cheap or missing air filters make the
air-flow sensor dirty, so it under-reads the air and the computer adds too little fuel.
Weak fuel pumps and clogged fuel filters (dirty or adulterated fuel speeds this up) also
starve the engine. Cheap checks first: listen for hissing at idle, look at the hoses and
the intake boot, and look at the air filter, before replacing sensors.
