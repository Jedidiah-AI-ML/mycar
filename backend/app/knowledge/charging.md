---
title: Charging system and battery voltage
codes: [P0562, P0563, P0560, P0620, P0622]
systems: [charging]
keywords: [alternator, battery, voltage, belt, terminal, charging]
---
Low system voltage usually means the alternator isn't charging enough (a worn alternator,
a slipping or worn drive belt) or the battery can't hold charge. Heat shortens battery
life, so batteries in Lagos often last 2-3 years. Corroded battery terminals and a poor
engine ground strap cause the same symptoms and are cheap to fix. If the voltage keeps
falling while driving, the car can stall when the battery runs flat.
