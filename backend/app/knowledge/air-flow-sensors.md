---
title: Air flow (MAF) and manifold pressure (MAP) sensors
codes: [P0100, P0101, P0102, P0103, P0104, P0105, P0106, P0107, P0108]
systems: [fuel_air]
keywords: [maf, map, air, flow, sensor, filter, dirty, vacuum]
---
The air-flow sensor tells the computer how much air enters the engine. Dust gets past worn
or poor-quality air filters and coats it, so it under-reads; cleaning it with a proper MAF
cleaner (without touching the wire) often fixes the code. Oiled performance air filters
can also coat it. An air leak between the sensor and the engine gives the same code, and
so can a cracked hose to the manifold pressure sensor.
